# Weapon audio

These recordings are bundled locally. Gameplay does not contact their hosts.
Each weapon has its own edited mix: onset trimming, pitch, EQ, transient, body,
mechanical action and tail. A calibre-family recording is not represented as an
authentic recording of every individual model. The Barrett sound is a designed
heavy-rifle mix, not a field recording of an M82A1.
